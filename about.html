About Me

A reader, writer, and emerging thinker navigating the space between two cultures.

Background

I'm Zilin Yuan, a first-year international student at Rutgers University. I come from Wuhan, China, where I attended a competitive key high school and spent three years preparing for the Gaokao (Chinese college entrance exam). That experience taught me resilience and grit — qualities that continue to shape how I approach new challenges.

Interests & Themes

Throughout this semester, my writing has explored the themes of nostalgia and escapism. I'm fascinated by how memories shape our identities and how cultural narratives get reshaped — whether through Disney's retellings of global folklore or the psychology behind why we play games. I bring a cross-cultural perspective, drawing on both Chinese literary tradition and Western academic discourse.

This Portfolio

This website is the final project for my EAP II: Academic Writing course. It serves as a multimodal showcase of my growth as a reader and writer — documenting the journey from personal narrative to analytical essay to research-based assessment. I plan to continue using this site to document my work through college.
